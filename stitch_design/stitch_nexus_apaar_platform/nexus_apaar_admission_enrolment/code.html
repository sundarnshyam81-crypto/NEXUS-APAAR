Single-Seat Cross Verification Registry Protocol

In compliance with National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and UGC guidelines, students holding an authenticated APAAR ID cannot maintain concurrent active degree enrolments in full-time programs across separate higher education institutions without formal dual-degree exemption approval.
